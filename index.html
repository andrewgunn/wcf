Who we are

Decentralised businesses, one shared stake

The WCF family is made up of decentralised businesses operating across retail and mail order, e-commerce fulfilment, logistics and leisure.

Each business has real autonomy, so our customers stay at the heart of everything we do. We want them to order from us again and recommend us to friends and family.

As an employee-owned business, every one of our employee owners can make a meaningful difference to our performance. They share in our success through an annual Employee Ownership Dividend and free WCF shares.

Our Vision

To hand WCF over to future generations of employees in a stronger financial position than when we inherited it, so they can benefit from its continued existence and resilience.

Our Mission

To create a workplace where every employee owner matters and has a unique opportunity to make a difference, with meaningful, diverse and interesting roles.

Our guiding principles

The six Cs

Our values decide whether we're a great place to work and a great company to do business with. Every employee owner commits to celebrate good behaviours and call out bad ones.

  • Champion

    Take responsibility

    • Learn from mistakes
    • Be open to feedback
    • Resolve issues promptly
    • Be resilient
    • Tackle difficult conversations
    • Follow processes

  • Celebration

    Recognise our wins

    • Show recognition
    • Praise good effort
    • Small successes count too
    • Be part of the team
    • Say thank you
    • Be kind to all

  • Curiosity

    Be better

    • Find a better way
    • Keep a fresh perspective
    • Be open to change
    • Be more efficient
    • Share ideas for improvement

  • Choose

    Do the right thing

    • Be frank but fair
    • Put our people first
    • Challenge wrong behaviours
