E-Commerce Fulfilment

Our 2 newest brands to the WCF Family are A1 Lawn and Progreen Weed Control Solutions, which we acquired as part of our acquisition strategy to find and integrate family businesses seeking good and sustainable succession for their businesses and their employees.

Both A1 Lawn and Progreen are e-commerce brands which were acquired from the families that started them. Both the A1 Lawn and Progreen brands offer a wide range of products to gardeners, landscapers, paddock owners and amenity firms. Our products include lawn, paddock and grass seed, fertiliser, weedkiller, pesticide and ground care and sell to both individual consumers and across the wider amenity sectors. We offer training in the specialist application of these products and soil analysis to ensure that customers are choosing the correct products for their needs.

We have a team of qualified BASIS Technical Sales Advisors to support our customers. Both brands have their own ecommerce websites, as well as a number of own brand products which are also sold through Amazon and EBay.

We operate efficient ecommerce fulfilment operations for both brands at our new distribution centre in Peterborough.
