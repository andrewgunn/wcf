WCF Pet and Equestrian

Our Team Care

Our team at WCF Pet & Equestrian are passionate about animals and offer unrivalled levels of product expertise. Our 8 stores across Northern England and Southern Scotland stock an extensive range of products aimed at those who live in a rural community or aspire to a country way of life. We stock feed, equipment, accessories, clothing, animal health and toys for the pet, equestrian, poultry, small holder and wild bird sectors. We consider our loyal customers, and their pets, part of the WCF family and support our local communities through charitable giving, sponsorship and product donations. 

WCF has developed a number of its own ranges of premium dog and cat feed which is retailed in store and online through our own website and via Amazon. Our products are made for us in the UK and contain only the finest ingredients. Lakes Heritage is our best-selling grain-free dog feed. 

We are working with all our manufacturers on product sustainability. We stock a wide range of eco-friendly products in store.
