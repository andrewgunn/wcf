Our Guiding Principles

Our Guiding Principles are our values and determine whether we are seen as a great place to work by our employee owners and a great company to do business with by our customers. Every employee owner commits to adopt and celebrate good behaviours and call out bad ones. We take responsibilty, recognise our wins and always strive to be better in what we do. We learn from our mistakes and be open to feedback whilst praising good effort and being kind to all. We question too. Is there a better process to do this? How can we be more efficient? We are always challenging and sharing ideas for improvements.

  • learn from mistakes
  • be open to feedback
  • resolve issues promptly
  • be resilient
  • tackle difficult conversations head on
  • follow processes
  • show recognition
  • praise good effort
  • acknowledge the small as well as the big successes
  • be part of the team
  • say thank you for a job well done
  • be kind to all
  • find a better way to do it
  • keep a fresh perspective
  • be curious and open to change
  • be more efficient
  • share ideas for improvements
  • act with honesty and integrity
  • be frank but fair
  • always put our people first
  • challenge wrong behaviours
  • listen to, support and participate
  • show an interest
  • be a positive team member
  • respect each others challenges
  • appreciate different points of view
  • recognise the big picture
  • compromise
  • know when to let go
  • give constructive feedback
  • work towards the same common goal
