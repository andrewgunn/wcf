Our Board

Executive Directors

Phil Murray 
Chief Executive Officer

Phil Murray joined WCF in April 2018 and was immediately appointed to the Board as Deputy Managing Director. He became our CEO in November 2024. A Chartered Accountant, Phil has held a number of Finance and Commercial Director positions in a number of sectors. Phil was President of the UK and Ireland Fuel Distributors Association (UKIFDA). UKIFDA is the trade association for the liquid fuels distribution industry and is playing a key role in the future fuels strategy as the UK transitions towards greener energy. Phil is also a member of Vistage,  a peer learning and mentoring program for senior executives. 

Andrew Mochrie
Chief Operating Officer

Andrew Mochrie was appointed Chief Operating Officer in November 2024, and subsequently appointed to the Board in June 2025. A Chartered Management Accountant, Andy has held Finance and Operations/Supply Chain Director roles across a number of sectors. Most recently Andy was Supply Chain & Customer Care Director at Matches Fashion. Prior to that he was Logistics & Supply Chain Director at the Newcastle-based, global fashion retailer, END. Andy holds an MBA and is a passionate advocate of the benefits of continuous learning and development.

 Pam Murray
 Company Secretary

Pam Murray is Company Secretary of WCF and was appointed to the Board in 2017. A Chartered Management Accountant, Pam joined WCF as Financial Controller 2015 and is now our Head of Finance. Previously Pam spent 27 years at Enesco, the UK’s largest manufacturer and distributor of giftware and collectables.

Non-Executive Chair

 Martin Ward

Now retired, Martin Ward was an Audit and Corporate Finance Partner with Dodd & Co Chartered Accountants. 


Martin has recently been appointed Chair of the Board, having been a Non-Executive Director for the past 18 years with WCF and also chairs the WCF Finance and Audit Committee. Martin joined the WCF Board in 2007. Martin advised entrepreneurial companies on their growth strategies and how to implement robust corporate reporting. He also works with Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW) as part of their Disciplinary Committee and was previously Vice Chair of the ICAEW Audit Registration Committee and a Member of the Audit Practices Board of the Financial Reporting Council (FRC).
