Pet & Equestrian

Our team at WCF Pet & Equestrian are passionate about animals and offer unrivalled levels of product expertise. Our 8 stores across Northern England and Southern Scotland stock an extensive range of products aimed at those who live in a rural community or aspire to a country way of life. We stock feed, equipment, accessories, clothing, animal health and toys for the pet, equestrian, poultry, small holder and wild bird sectors. We consider our loyal customers, and their pets, part of the WCF family and support our local communities through charitable giving, sponsorship and product donations.

WCF has developed a number of its own ranges of premium dog and cat feed which is retailed in store and online through our own website and via Amazon. Our products are made for us in the UK and contain only the finest ingredients. Lakes Heritage is our best-selling grain-free dog feed.

We are working with all our manufacturers on product sustainability. We stock a wide range of eco-friendly products in store and participate in the Terra-Cycle plastic recycling scheme allowing our customers to return their used pouches and plastic packaging to store for re-cycling.

Leisure

Our objective is to develop a portfolio of 5-star camping and glamping sites in amazing locations to give our guests an unforgettable experience whatever the weather and allow them to create long-lasting memories for all the family. WCF currently operates 3 sites, the award-winning Hadrian’s Wall 5-star site Herding Hill Farm in Northumberland, Drummohr Camping and Glamping Site close to Edinburgh and the recently acquired Longnor Wood in the heart of the Peak District. We also have Herding Campers camper van hire.

Our dog-friendly sites offer a mix of touring, camping, glamping, holiday lodge and self-catering accommodation. We have development programs in place to further improve the facilities and premiuimise the accommodations. We employ a mix of on-site wardens and individuals from the local communities to provide the best possible working experience for our teams and offer a personalised guest experience. Longnor Wood is an adults-only site.

We work actively to promote the attractions, events and amenities of the local communities and encourage people to visit these wonderful areas.

Apparel

WCF started our Apparel mail order business in 1989 and now operates a portfolio of 4 clothing brands for the mature market – Country Collection, featuring James Meade, The Classic Boutique and Bella di Notte, which we acquired from the founder in December 2023. We aim to be the UK’s most trusted retailer by mail order of high-quality, long-lasting, stylish and timeless classics and co-ordinates to the over 65’s. We believe that everyone should have the opportunity to look their best no matter what their age or gender.

All our own garments and our catalogues are designed in the UK by our in-house teams and manufactured for us by a small number of family-run businesses both in the UK and overseas. Whilst an increasing proportion of our sales orders are now received on-line, we offer dedicated customer service telephone contacts for those customers who prefer to chat to us before placing their order. All products are despatched from our warehouse in Brampton.

We have many customer recruitment and retention strategies both on and offline and have our own dedicated marketing and data analysis teams to ensure that we are placing the right products in front of the right customers in the right places at the right time.

Oil Distribution

WCF are one of the Top 10 independent oil distributors in the UK, operating around 80 tankers from 12 fuel storage depots from Norfolk to Lincolnshire to Humberside to Northumberland and from Lancashire to Cumbria. Our tanker fleet ranges from baby 4-wheelers for the tightest of access through to articulated vehicles and are fitted with on-truck computers to facilitate deliveries for our drivers. We use automated routing software and the expertise of our transport teams to minimise miles travelled and ensure maximum drop efficiency.

Our 4 fuels businesses operate independently in their own geographies but frequently collaborate to share best practice and benchmark their performance. Our fuels customer base ranges from domestic heating oil customers in rural locations, through agriculture and farmers, a small number of retail filling stations, hauliers and commercial customers. Customers can order on-line, by text or in person by speaking to one of our customer services teams.

We are working closely with our industry body UKIFDA as the UK transitions from fossil fuels into greener forms of energy and have recently started trials of a green liquid fuel as part of a wider government consultation. We have recently started our own Driver Academy to develop our own talent pool of future drivers in response to the widely reported industry shortages.

e-Commerce Fulfillment

Our 2 newest brands to the WCF Family are A1 Lawn and Progreen Weed Control Solutions, e-commerce brands which were recently acquired from the families that started them. Both brands offer a wide range of product for lawn, weedkiller and ground care and sell to both individual consumers and across the wider amenity sectors. We also offer training in the specialist application of these products. Both brands have their own websites, as well as a number of own brand products which are also sold through Amazon.
