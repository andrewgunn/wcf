About WCF

The WCF family is made up of decentralised businesses operating across the retail and mail order, e-commerce fulfilment, logistics and leisure sectors. WCF has been in operation for over 110 years and has a strong and rich legacy.

Each business is given significant autonomy so that our customers are at the heart of everything we do, and will want to order from us again and recommend us to their friends and family.

We are an employee-owned business. Every one of our 350+ employee owners has the opportunity to make a meaningful difference to our performance, and shares in our success through an annual Employee Ownership Dividend and free WCF shares.

Our Legacy

Established in 1911, WCF has a strong and diverse heritage. Many of our customers still refer to us as "West Cumberland Farmers", despite us changing our name 35 years ago, which shows the strength of our connection with the communities we serve.

Over the past century we have moved from the agricultural roots of West Cumberland Farmers into the diverse organisation we are today, while staying true to our values.
