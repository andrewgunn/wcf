WCF Sustainability Statement

September 2026

WCF is committed to reducing the environmental impact of its operations and sourcing products responsibly. Across the Group, we are taking practical steps to reduce emissions, use resources more efficiently and support responsible business practices throughout our supply chain.

Understanding our carbon footprint

We are working with carbon management consultancy My Carbon to measure and understand our carbon footprint, including Scope 3 emissions associated with our wider value chain. This will inform our roadmap towards carbon neutrality and help us prioritise the areas where we can make the greatest reductions.

Our priority is to reduce emissions wherever possible. Carbon offsetting may also form part of our approach, particularly within our Fuel businesses. We will monitor and report emissions across the Group to assess progress and guide further action.

Supporting the transition to lower carbon fuels

We recognise the environmental impact of distributing fossil fuels and our responsibility to support the transition to lower carbon alternatives. Many customers continue to rely on these fuels to heat their homes and operate their businesses. While meeting those needs, we are working to reduce the emissions associated with our distribution activities.

We expect demand for fossil fuels to decline over time and are diversifying the Group through investment in areas such as leisure and e-commerce fulfilment. We also anticipate a growing role for liquid biofuels and will adapt our logistics operations as the market develops.

We work with government, industry and customers to explore and implement lower carbon alternatives. We participated in a UK trial assessing the use of hydrotreated vegetable oil (HVO) as a replacement for kerosene in domestic heating. Through UKIFDA, the UK and Ireland Fuel Distributors Association, we continue to campaign for changes to fuel duty that would make HVO more affordable for customers. Our Fuels businesses can supply both red and white HVO and will continue to support customers through the transition.

Improving delivery efficiency

We plan delivery routes to minimise tanker mileage and encourage customers to participate in buying groups, select efficient delivery routes and place orders in advance. We generally schedule deliveries to make the best use of each journey, with average mileage per delivery used as a key performance measure. Our tankers are dispatched fully loaded.

Our diesel fleet is less than seven years old, meets Euro VI emissions standards and uses AdBlue to reduce exhaust pollutants. Regular servicing and maintenance help keep vehicles operating efficiently.

Unless a specialist carrier is required, we use Royal Mail for home shopping deliveries. Its delivery network, including deliveries made on foot, supports our efforts to reduce the environmental impact of customer orders.

Reducing packaging and paper use

We aim to minimise packaging while ensuring products reach customers in good condition. Across our retail and home shopping businesses, we have reduced unnecessary packaging and single-use plastics. Garment bags are used only where needed to protect clothing and are recyclable. We have introduced paper despatch bags that customers can reuse for returns and removed unnecessary components such as swing tags and collar holders.

Catalogues are sent without wrapping wherever possible, with paper wraps used when required. We manage mailing lists to reduce unnecessary distribution and work with suppliers to reduce paper consumption and paper weight. Digital marketing also helps us reduce our reliance on printed mailings.

We have re-engineered the packaging across our own brand pet food range to provide a more sustainable solution. The packaging is biodegradable and reusable while providing an effective barrier against moisture loss to preserve the quality of the food. We are also working with major branded suppliers to reduce shrink wrap on pallet deliveries.

Building a responsible supply chain

We use UK suppliers wherever practicable, while recognising that products such as textiles and pet accessories are also sourced internationally. All our own brand pet food is manufactured in the UK. For overseas orders, we consolidate shipments and prioritise sea freight over air freight to reduce transport emissions.

Wherever possible, we seek fabrics from sustainable or recyclable sources. Our own brand of dog food uses by-products from the food industry, making productive use of materials that might otherwise go to waste.

Sourcing clothing responsibly

Bella di Notte and Country Collection source clothing from manufacturers across the world. We require the businesses we work with to respect workers’ rights and provide safe, fair working conditions.

We ensure that the manufacturing sites of all new suppliers supplying our clothing are independently audited using SMETA, the Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit methodology, which assesses labour standards and health and safety. We review audit findings and expect suppliers to address identified issues. These assessments inform our sourcing decisions and support improvements in working conditions across our supply chain.

Reducing waste

Our apparel businesses focus on quality and enduring design, with garments made to last. Repair and alteration services, together with a free lifetime product guarantee, support this approach. Samples and unsold items are offered to employees free of charge before remaining items are donated to charity.

Pet food approaching its expiry date is donated to animal rescue centres and local charities. Our in-store nutritional advisers help customers choose suitable products and understand appropriate feeding quantities. A range of pack sizes also helps customers buy the amount they need and avoid waste.

We have reduced paper use through digital reporting, shared electronic filing systems and processes that provide customers with electronic correspondence, delivery notes, invoices and statements. All sites take an active approach to recycling and reducing energy consumption.

Improving the efficiency of our sites

Across our businesses, energy efficiency measures include solar panels, LED lighting and automatic lighting controls. We have installed solar panels at our head office and provided electric vehicle charging facilities across the Group. We also encourage employee owners to use video conferencing where appropriate to reduce unnecessary travel.
