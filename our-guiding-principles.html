Our Guiding Principles

Our Guiding Principles are our values and determine whether we are seen as a great place to work by our employee owners and a great company to do business with by our customers. Every employee owner commits to adopt and celebrate good behaviours and call out bad ones. We take responsibility, recognise our wins and always strive to be better in what we do. We learn from our mistakes and be open to feedback whilst praising good effort and being kind to all. We question too. Is there a better process to do this? How can we be more efficient? We are always challenging and sharing ideas for improvements.

  • Champion

    Take responsibility

    • Learn from mistakes
    • Be open to feedback
    • Resolve issues promptly
    • Be resilient
    • Tackle difficult conversations
    • Follow processes

  • Celebration

    Recognise our wins

    • Show recognition
    • Praise good effort
    • Small successes count too
    • Be part of the team
    • Say thank you
    • Be kind to all

  • Curiosity

    Be better

    • Find a better way
    • Keep a fresh perspective
    • Be open to change
    • Be more efficient
    • Share ideas for improvement

  • Choose

    Do the right thing

    • Act with honesty and integrity
    • Be frank but fair
    • Put our people first
    • Challenge wrong behaviours

  • Collaboration

    Communicate

    • Listen, support and participate
    • Show an interest
    • Be a positive team member
    • Respect each other's challenges

  • Camaraderie

    Work together

    • Appreciate other viewpoints
    • See the big picture
    • Compromise
    • Know when to let go
    • Give constructive feedback
    • Share a common goal
